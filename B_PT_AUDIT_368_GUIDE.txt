limit. It does not train, change the checkpoint, resume the failed job,
cancel other jobs, or modify the core repository. Submission checks all
required input paths and refuses a duplicate queued/running audit.

Input checkpoint:
/scratch/mdraihk/ptflow/active_pt/B_PT_Full_20260924_030649_u9mui_x5/job/B_PT_Full/checkpoints/state_00000368.pt

The job loads raw generator, potential, and scale weights, holds epsilon at
the checkpoint value, and compares 72 settings:
  Refinement steps: 0, 8, 32, 64
  Covariance: learned diagonal, identity (relative to 2*epsilon)
  Defensive mixture alpha: 0.10, 0.50, 0.75
  Importance draws K: 16, 64, 128
There are 32 fixed input noise/label pairs and three proposal repeats.
K comparisons use nested antithetic pairs. Generator CFG is 1.0, matching
the potential-training proposal convention, not the FID evaluation CFG.

variability, and refinement residuals, with conditional/null/all cohorts.
per_sample.csv retains individual observations; manifest.json records the
protocol. The relative residual always uses the original generator
displacement in its denominator for comparability across refinement depths.

This is proposal screening, not a FID or normalized-likelihood report.
