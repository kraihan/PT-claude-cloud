PT-FLOW B/2: FRESH 30K RUN, W-FLOW WEIGHTS ONLY, TWO H200 GPUs

This is an isolated experimental recovery recipe. It starts at step zero.
The W-Flow EMA generator weights initialize the generator and its EMA. Its
200,000-step counter, optimizer moments and PT state are NOT loaded.
The generator is trainable. The potential and scale networks start fresh.
There is no fixed teacher loss against the original W-Flow checkpoint.

RECIPE
- ImageNet-1k 256x256, cached 32x32x4 latents, base model/MAE configuration.
- Generator B architecture unchanged; residual=False to match W-Flow weights.
- 30,000 new optimizer iterations, checkpoints every 1,000.
- 128 global label groups x 64 generated samples per group = 8,192 samples
  per generator update. Each of two ranks handles 64 label groups, split into
  32 accumulation chunks: 2 groups / 128 generated images per microbatch.
  IMPORTANT: accumulation divides a batch here; it does NOT multiply it.
  Your earlier train_batch_size=32 recipe generated 2,048 samples/update.
  This run processes FOUR TIMES that earlier batch per optimizer iteration.
  No speed improvement is promised by this full-PT recovery recipe.
- Generator BF16 setting is inherited; generator remat enabled.
- Real MAE features stream per accumulation group: train.feature_chunk_size=16.
  Feature forwards use feature.chunk_size=16 and feature.checkpoint=true.
  The GPU feature cache is disabled. Both settings are needed: chunking an
  MAE forward alone still concatenates all real features for the entire batch.
  The earlier kit omitted streaming and OOMed before the first update on H200.
- Potential and scale FP32, potential remat enabled.
- Potential/data batch 32 per rank, K=16, scale_K=4, potential chunks of 16.
- Full prox gradient, including input Hessians; four sampled points per
  generator microbatch, prox_norm=none. No silent detach fallback.
- Generator peak LR 2e-5, warmup 1000, cosine to 30000.
- Potential/scale peak LR 2e-5, warmup 500, cosine to 30000.
- Potential and scale optimizer steps are attempted every iteration,
  including when the measured health is broken.
- Generator FID-50K, CFG 1.2, EMA, every 10k steps. These are Mode-A samples;
  low Mode-A FID alone does not validate the potential or a likelihood bound.

The first 1000 iterations let the fresh potential train before the generator
receives its prox penalty. Then lambda ramps over 5000 iterations. Epsilon
anneals over 20000 iterations after its 1000-iteration warmup.
The full 30k target is conditional on finite gradients and estimator recovery.

WHAT CHANGED TO ADDRESS THE FAILURE
The old controller froze potential AND scale optimization when ESS was broken,
froze epsilon progression, and left the prox term at zero. For the supplied
checkpoint, only 158 potential optimizer updates had occurred by step 1000.
Its prox warmup had not finished, but its stalled epsilon counter and frozen
optimizer indicated a separate estimator/controller problem.

A fresh zero potential has identity prox; an image-producing pretrained
generator is generally not that prox. The new importance sampler therefore
chooses the lower-energy start between the generator output and input noise,
then takes eight backtracked descent steps on phi(y)+||y-x||^2/2. Sampling AND
proposal density evaluation use that same refined mean. This can improve
proposal matching; it does not guarantee good high-dimensional importance
sampling or certify convexity/normalization.

A temporary gradient calibration term trains phi against the CURRENT frozen
generator pairs: grad phi(m) ~= x-m. Its weight is 0.1 through step 1000 and
fades to zero at step 5000. This is an additional empirical objective; a
general generator need not be representable by a scalar prox map. It is not
an unchanged implementation of the paper's objective or a fixed W-Flow teacher.

Policy active_recovery_v1 advances epsilon/prox by iteration, independently of
health. The real corrected ESS and health state remain visible. An annealing
epsilon or positive lambda alone does NOT prove PT is learning successfully.
The code reports refined proposal ESS and unrefined generator proposal ESS
separately. High refined ESS does not prove that Mode-A transport is a prox map.

After iteration 1500, 500 consecutive broken-health iterations cause a saved
checkpoint plus run/PT_FAILED.txt and a failed job. It will NOT spend the rest
of 30k pretending recovery succeeded. Nonfinite losses/gradients fail directly.
No learned architecture, threshold, or new recipe can promise the lowest FID.

WATCH THESE METRICS TO JUDGE ACTUAL RECOVERY
- pt/potential_updated and pt/scale_updated: should remain 1.
- pt/lambda_prox: actual weight used in the update (positive after warmup).
- pt/loss_prox and pt/prox_resid_rel: generator/potential agreement.
- pt/control_ess, pt/ess_ema, pt/health: measured refined estimator health.
- pt/generator_proposal_control_ess: unrefined generator proposal diagnostic.
- pt/refine_residual_rms: quality of the local proposal refinement.
- pt/loss_scale, pt/loss_alignment, pt/g_norm_potential: optimizer diagnostics.
- FID over training: check that generator quality does not deteriorate.
Control ESS=(raw ESS/K - 1/K)/(1-1/K). For K=16, the raw floor is 1/16.
Health thresholds apply to control ESS, not raw ESS/K. These sample estimates
do not themselves certify theoretical likelihood normalization or bias.

REQUEUE
Ten minutes before the 72h limit, the batch script requests a checkpoint.
The trainer saves at the next optimizer boundary, then the script requeues.
Requeue resumes THIS NEW RUN's generator, PT state, optimizers and schedules.
It does not reread W-Flow as a resume checkpoint. Failed estimator checks do
not automatically requeue. Exact sample-for-sample reproduction after requeue
is not guaranteed because the memory bank/data loader are refilled.
