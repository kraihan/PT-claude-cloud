Job name: B_PT_scale368

What runs:
  1. Read raw checkpoint state_00000368.pt from the confirmed B_PT_Full folder.
  2. Freeze the generator and potential; hold checkpoint epsilon fixed.
  3. Prepare 128 fit pairs and 64 independent held-out noise/label pairs.
     Refine their proposal means for 64 steps with the frozen potential.
  4. Evaluate the existing scale on the held-out inputs.
  5. Update ONLY the copied scale network in memory for 500 steps.
     AdamW LR=0.0003, weight decay=0, batch=4, eight antithetic draws.
     Objective is reverse KL up to additive constants, divided by dimension.
     No importance-weight clipping or health-threshold changes are used.
  6. Evaluate the fitted scale on exactly the same held-out inputs and
     sampling seeds. Report K=16/64/128, alpha=0.10/0.75 and variance
     multipliers=0.25/0.5/1/2, before and after fitting.

The fit never uses the held-out inputs, their metrics, or any early-stopping
selection. Primary comparison is multiplier=1 and alpha=0.10. The multiplier
grid is exploratory. Conditional and null-conditioned results are separate.
The conditional/null split is prespecified from the configured dropout rate.

The generator/potential are not retrained. The source checkpoint and core
files are not overwritten. The script verifies source checkpoint metadata,
generator/potential parameter versions, absent parameter gradients, and
unchanged schedule after running. The candidate scale is saved separately
as scale_candidate.pt, which is NOT a resumable PT-Flow checkpoint.
